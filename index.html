    How to Prepare



  • Stretch before playing, ESPECIALLY your legs and arms, you will be using that a LOT.
  • Drink enough water before going out there on the court, you need to stay hydrated afterall!
  • Be sure to conserve as much as stamina as you can for your game, you can't play when tired!

    Beware


  • Always keep your arms straight! Otherwise, the ball might go elsewhere instead of straight
  • Utilize your legs when recieving, push forward and upward as if you'll jump so more power is put into it.
  • When serving, keep in mind of how strong you hit the ball and where you hit the ball,
    sometimes it could go too far, sometimes it could be too weak.
